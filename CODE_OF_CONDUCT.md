# Code of Conduct

We want PHP2Shell Exposure Check to be a respectful and constructive project for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific in discussions and reviews.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Accept constructive feedback and focus on the project's defensive purpose.
- Protect privacy: do not post credentials, private data, or identifiable scan results for systems you do not control.
- Keep technical examples safe, bounded, and reproducible with local fixtures or reserved example domains.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized attention, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable. Do not use project discussions to coordinate unauthorized testing or disclose unpatched third-party systems.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation, or ban accounts for conduct that violates this policy. Report concerns privately to the project maintainer, [@DevCop95](https://github.com/DevCop95). Reports will be handled discreetly and reviewed fairly.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.
